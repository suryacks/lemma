Questions

Which courses will you cover?

We're starting with math-heavy college courses: calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability and physics. Tell us your course after you join and we'll prioritize the most-requested ones.

Are the lessons accurate?

Every lesson script is checked for correctness, and we work each example by hand before it ships. If you spot a mistake, tell us and we fix it.

How long are lessons?

The samples run about 3 minutes. Bigger topics are split into chapters, so you can jump straight to the part you need.

How much will it cost?

Free during early access. At launch we expect a free tier, Pro at $19.99/month or $119.99/year, and a $9.99 finals-week pass. Early members can lock in a founding price of $49 for the first year.

What happens when I join?

You get one email when lessons for your course are ready. No spam, and you can ask to be removed anytime.
